// Systems online

Every corner of the screen, one design

From the health bar under your thumb to the profession window you open once a week, everything shares the same surfaces, fonts and accents.

UNIT FRAMES

Four frame styles

Player, target, focus, pet, party, raid, boss and arena frames in four styles: skull-art Morph, classic Norm, clean Eclipse, or Dark Matter with its power ring.

COMBAT

Bars & nameplates

Configurable action bars with fading and geometry controls, plus nameplates, cast bars, auras and tooltips tuned for fights.

INVENTORY

Unified bags

Built in-house: category headings, search, favorites, quality accents and a two-click Sell gray that never touches favorites.

METER

Asphyxia-DPS

A themed face for WoW Forever's native damage meter, with compact and split layouts and class icons. Toggle with /adps.

RAID

Raid Control

Target markers, a marker wheel, ready-check display and pull and break timers, ready when you lead.

SKIN ENGINE

Blizzard windows

The map, quest log, professions, flight map, mail and more pick up Asphyxia panels and Ironclad type automatically.

// Frame styles

Four ways to wear it

Pick a unit frame style in /asp > Unit Frames. Each one keeps your other settings, so you can switch between them.

STYLE 01 · CLASSIC

Norm

  • Name and level bar sits above the frame, clear of the portrait.
  • Full portrait health bar with health and percent in the corner.
  • Full-width power bar directly underneath.
STYLE 02 · SIGNATURE

Asphyxia-Morph

  • Segmented health in class-inspired colour, readable at a glance mid-pull.
  • Skull emblem on every frame, mirrored for your target.
  • Lightning orb and an angular power bar tucked under the health.
STYLE 03 · CLEAN

Eclipse

  • Bracket rails frame the health bar for a sharper outline.
  • Full portrait health bar with health and percent in the corner.
  • Inset power bar centred under the frame.
STYLE 04 · ALPHA

Dark Matter

  • Circular power ring on the outer edge, filling clockwise or counter-clockwise.
  • 3D portrait fills the health area, with numeric health in the corner.
  • Solid black header with name and level.

// Deployment

Install in five steps

Keep your WTF folder when you upgrade and your profiles carry over.

  1. Close WoW Forever completely.
  2. Back up your AddOns and WTF folders.
  3. Download Asphyxia-UI.zip and extract it.
  4. Replace the old folder in Interface/AddOns with the new Asphyxia-UI folder.
  5. Enable Asphyxia-UI in the addon list and log in.
