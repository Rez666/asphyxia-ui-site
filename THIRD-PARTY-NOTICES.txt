ARMORY GEARSCORE CALCULATION (added 2026-11-18)

The compact Classic/Forever GearScore calculation in Modules/Armory.lua adapts
the coefficient and slot-weight calculation in TacoTip's gearscore.lua.
TacoTip is based on the original GearScoreLite by Mirrikat45 and others.
Source: https://github.com/ssdeanx/TacoTip-Gearscore-TBC/blob/1f9222276683b97bfbc0333a55461de97739795a/gearscore.lua
